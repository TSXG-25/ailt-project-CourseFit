A good week, one step at a time

  1. Bring your shortlist.

    Paste your courses, section options and preferences. There is no course or section count limit. Midnight catalogue slots ending at 08:00 or 08:20 are treated as online classes with no fixed meeting.

  2. Answer once, if needed.

    Any essential questions are grouped into one batch of up to five. Your answers go straight into generation, with no extra confirmation.

  3. Find your fit, then fine-tune.

    Compare clash-free options and describe any adjustment in your own words. Only timetables satisfying every hard constraint are shown; each soft preference earns one point.

CourseFit does not choose courses for you, enrol you, or check seats. Times repeat weekly in the local times you provide; dates, alternating weeks and travel time are not modelled.

Plans stay in server memory for up to two hours and disappear when the server restarts. Large searches have a time budget; incomplete searches are labelled. Model explanations are optional; preference checks come directly from the solver.
